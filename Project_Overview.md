# Project Overview

**Title:** Supply Chain Efficiency & Inventory Optimization Dashboard

**Objective:** Use Power BI to monitor sample supply-chain indicators and investigate inventory, supplier, regional, forecasting, and cost issues.

## Core questions
- Which products show stockout or reorder risk?
- Which suppliers have weaker delivery performance?
- How do warehouse and regional indicators compare?
- How closely do forecast demand and actual demand align?
- Which categories show stronger inventory movement proxies?
- What operational actions could improve efficiency and reduce avoidable cost?

## Planned Power BI pages
1. Executive Overview
2. Inventory Optimization
3. Supplier & Procurement
4. Demand & Replenishment

The dataset is synthetic and observation-level. Treat KPI results as exploratory proxies unless validated against transaction-level source data.
